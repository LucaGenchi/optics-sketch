Two-photon lensless endoscope with a double-clad tapered MCF

By @LucaGenchi · August 18, 2026 · source discussion

Reconstructs the experimental layout in Figure 2 of Genchi et al.: a 920 nm, 180 fs, 80 MHz source drives a reflective SLM whose phase-only gratings and lenslet arrays form core-matched beamlets. A qualitative telescope train couples them through the paper's Nikon NA 0.75 objective into the tapered multi-core fiber; proximal spectral separation routes returned fluorescence to a PMT, while the paper's 20×, NA 0.45 distal calibration telescope images the fiber end on a CMOS camera. The fiber is a single schematic path annotated with the reported excitation NA 0.22 and collection NA 0.48. OpticalSetup does not simulate coherent wavefront shaping, transmission-matrix calibration, Fermat-spiral cores, taper or polymer-cladding physics, multimode fluorescence collection, two-photon excitation, diffraction/PSF, Strehl ratio, memory effect, or calibrated power. Unreported telescope dimensions and the drawn distal forward-detection branch are illustrative.
